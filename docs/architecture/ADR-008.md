# ADR-008: Shadow Parity and Readiness Reporting

## Purpose

Record how repository foundations compare observations without entering the
execution path.

## Decision

Captured left/right observations may be coordinated in `shadow-only` mode and
reduced to parity evidence. Reports are canonical, content-digested, identify the
Phase 8 schema, and declare `evidence_only=true`. Execution authority remains the
existing runtime.

Evidence includes `agent/src/integration/shadow/coordinator.py`, parity helpers,
and `agent/src/integration/shadow/report.py`.

## Alternatives

- Inline dual execution with automatic fallback.
- Cutover based on a single comparison result.
- Human-readable reports without deterministic evidence.

## Tradeoffs

Shadow comparison provides low-risk evidence but cannot establish complete
behavioral parity unless capture coverage and acceptance thresholds are defined.

## Consequences

Readiness reports inform an audit; they do not approve migration. A NO-GO audit
cannot be overridden by report generation alone.

## Scope

Phase 8 shadow integration and evidence reporting only.
