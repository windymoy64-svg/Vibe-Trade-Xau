# ADR-011: Evidence-Only Verification Manifests

## Purpose

Record the approved audit artifact that binds evidence, policy snapshot metadata,
and verification results.

## Decision

`VerificationManifest` is immutable and schema-versioned. It records the evidence
identifier and digest, policy identifier/version/digest, integrity and authenticity
outcomes, reason, and timezone-aware UTC verification timestamp. Its identifier is
a domain-separated SHA-256 digest of canonical bound metadata; its artifact digest
is SHA-256 over the complete canonical manifest. Construction rejects incomplete,
internally inconsistent, authoritative, or non-evidence-only records.

Historical replay uses the stored timestamp and caller-supplied exact evidence and
policy snapshot, recreates the manifest, and compares complete digests.

## Consequences

Audit artifacts detect changes to any bound verification metadata and reproduce
without wall-clock or implicit-current-policy state. Replay still requires access
to the referenced evidence and policy snapshot.

## Scope

Foundation Hardening Sprint 6 only. No execution, persistence backend, signing,
migration, or runtime authority is introduced.
