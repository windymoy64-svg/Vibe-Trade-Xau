# ADR-013: Evidence Analytics and Policy Insights

## Purpose

Derive deterministic analytics from approved Foundation, Phase 10 observation, and
Phase 11 archive artifacts without introducing runtime behavior or authority.

## Decision

Phase 12 consists of immutable analytics projections and pure aggregation functions.
Evidence quality combines completeness, authenticity, verification volume, and
weighted latency. Session trends use canonical `(opened_at, session_id)` ordering.
Policy insights group verification manifests by exact policy ID, version, and digest.
Replay analytics aggregate caller-supplied immutable outcomes and never initiate a
replay. Health reports combine observer coverage, verification results, and Phase 11
archive-chain verification. A composed dashboard contains these artifacts without
adding commands or control surfaces.

All percentages use deterministic integer floor division and return zero for empty
denominators. Every analytics contract is frozen, strict-canonical-JSON compatible,
non-authoritative, evidence-only, and declares the existing runtime as execution
authority.

## Boundaries

Phase 12 has no execution, enforcement, trading, broker/exchange, scheduler,
persistence, database, migration, signing, consensus, or authority-transfer behavior.
It consumes caller-supplied approved artifacts and does not discover, fetch, replay,
store, mutate, or act upon evidence.

## Consequences

Analytics quality is bounded by input completeness and correctness. Integer rates
lose sub-percentage precision. Replay timestamps are descriptive caller-supplied
evidence rather than external attestations. Policy compliance summarizes recorded
verification outcomes; it does not enforce policy.

## Scope

Level 14, Phase 12 only. Phase 13 is not authorized by this decision.
