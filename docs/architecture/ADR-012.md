# ADR-012: Immutable Observation Archive and Deterministic Audit Chain

## Purpose

Retain Phase 10 observation evidence in deterministic, independently verifiable
archive descriptors without introducing storage or runtime behavior.

## Decision

`ObservationArchiveEntry` binds a sealed Phase 10 observation session identifier,
complete session digest, and caller-supplied UTC archive timestamp. Entries are
immutable and use domain-separated SHA-256 identifiers over strict canonical JSON.

`ObservationArchiveBatch` sorts entries by deterministic entry identifier, rejects
duplicate entries and sessions, and binds the complete ordered content into a batch
identifier and digest. `AuditChainLink` records a contiguous sequence, batch identity,
complete batch digest, and the previous batch digest. The genesis predecessor is a
64-zero sentinel. `AuditChain` validates all linkage during construction and exposes
fail-closed integrity verification.

Archive verification compares caller-supplied batches with ordered chain links and,
when supplied, an expected ordered batch-ID manifest. It reports omission,
duplication, reordering, and corruption independently. Dashboard models derive entry
count, batch count, chain integrity, replay coverage, and health from this evidence.

## Boundaries

The archive is a value-object and verification layer only. It has no persistence
backend, database, scheduler, execution API, broker/exchange/trading dependency,
signing service, consensus mechanism, migration behavior, or authority-transfer
path. The existing runtime remains the only execution authority.

## Consequences

Digest chains detect mutation but do not provide signatures, external timestamping,
durability, or availability. Omission relative to an intended archive requires the
caller to provide the expected ordered batch identifiers; a chain cannot prove the
existence of evidence that was never committed to its input.

## Scope

Level 13, Phase 11 only. Phase 12 is not authorized by this decision.
