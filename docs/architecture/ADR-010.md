# ADR-010: Versioned Trust-Policy Snapshots

## Purpose

Record the approved trust-policy lifecycle used for historically reproducible
evidence authenticity verification.

## Decision

Repository trust policy is represented by immutable snapshots identified by a
normalized policy identifier and positive integer version. Each snapshot records
a timezone-aware UTC effective interval and a canonical SHA-256 policy digest.
Snapshot collections reject duplicate `(policy_id, version)` keys and support
exact historical lookup. Authenticity verification receives a concrete snapshot,
records its identifier, version, and digest, and fails closed when the snapshot is
not effective at the verification time.

## Consequences

Historical verification does not depend on an implicit current policy. Replaying
the same evidence against the same snapshot and timestamp is deterministic.
Callers must retain or supply the exact historical snapshot.

## Scope

Foundation Hardening Sprint 5 only. These records are repository-local evidence
contracts. They provide no signing, persistence, enforcement, execution authority,
or migration mechanism.
