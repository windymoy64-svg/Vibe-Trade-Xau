# ADR-018: Evidence-only Decision Evaluation Metrics

## Status

Pending architecture review.

## Context

Level 21 derives deterministic aggregate evidence from approved Level 20 reports.

## Decision

Total count is the number of supplied comparison artifacts. Agreement,
disagreement, and indeterminate ratios are integer floor percentages over total count,
returning zero when total count is zero.

Coverage means the number and integer floor percentage of supplied artifacts whose
classification is determinate (`agreement` or `disagreement`). Indeterminate artifacts
are uncovered. Coverage does **not** measure source-event capture, production traffic,
time windows, population representativeness, or operational monitoring completeness.

Distribution counts and ratios partition the supplied artifacts by the declared
dimension. Trend points are ordered by `(opened_at, session_id)`. Trend deltas are the
last point's count minus the first point's count for each classification; with fewer
than two points they do not establish a temporal trend. Caller-supplied timestamps are
descriptive evidence, not clock attestations.

## Public contract versioning and boundaries

The metrics baseline follows ADR-015. Changes to denominator, floor rounding,
coverage eligibility, dimensions, ordering, delta direction, canonicalization, or
digest behavior are breaking and require an explicit version and architecture review.
Metrics describe supplied evidence only. They are not SLOs, forecasts, recommendations,
quality gates, authority signals, or proof of capture completeness. No execution,
enforcement, persistence, scheduling, migration, or Level 24 behavior is authorized.
