# ADR-006: Observation-Only Lifecycle and Health

## Purpose

Record how lifecycle, health, and resource state are represented without
orchestrating runtime transitions.

## Decision

The foundation models lifecycle transitions, health snapshots, resource budgets,
and resource accounting as validated observations. Runtime supervision composes
those values into a non-executable observation. Validation can identify invalid
transitions or unhealthy states but does not apply a transition or remediate a
workload.

Evidence is located in `agent/src/aios/contracts/lifecycle.py` and
`agent/src/aios/runtime/{lifecycle,health,resources,supervisor}.py`.

## Alternatives

- Embed state transitions in model constructors.
- Couple health checks directly to restart/termination behavior.
- Use unstructured metrics dictionaries.

## Tradeoffs

Separating observation from action improves determinism and safety but leaves
operational orchestration outside this foundation.

## Consequences

Lifecycle and health results are reportable facts or proposals. They do not
change the existing runtime state.

## Scope

Phase 6 observation model. No scheduler, reconciler, remediation, or enforcement.
