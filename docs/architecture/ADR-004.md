# ADR-004: Content-Addressed Provenance

## Purpose

Record how research runtime resolution and supporting evidence are made
deterministic and verifiable.

## Decision

Runtime manifests pin resolved dependencies by resource, version, and SHA-256
digest. The Research Runtime Resolution Manifest combines runtime, capability,
isolation, health, and evidence contracts into canonical JSON with a stable
digest. Evidence must refer to the manifest or a resolved dependency. The RRM is
research-only and rejects authoritative mode.

Evidence includes `agent/src/aios/contracts/runtime_manifest.py` and
`agent/src/aios/provenance/manifest.py`.

## Alternatives

- Mutable run metadata with no canonical representation.
- Name-only dependency references.
- Provenance inferred retrospectively from logs.

## Tradeoffs

Digest verification requires canonical serialization and careful schema
evolution. It improves reproducibility but does not prove that execution occurred
as described.

## Consequences

Research observations can be linked to pinned inputs. Provenance remains
evidence and does not authorize or launch a run.

## Scope

Phase 4 provenance contracts and integrity helpers only.
